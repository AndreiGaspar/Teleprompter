# Lines in (parentheses) or [brackets] are stage directions, shown but never waited on.

[A kitchen, early morning. ANNA stands at the window.]

ANNA: You're up early.
BOB: Couldn't sleep. The storm kept me awake.
ANNA: It stopped an hour ago.
(Bob joins her at the window.)
BOB: Then why is the garden still shaking?
NARRATOR: Neither of them spoke of the noise from the cellar.
ANNA: Did you hear that?
BOB: I heard nothing.
ANNA: Liar.
[Pause. A door creaks.]
BOB: Fine. I heard it.
