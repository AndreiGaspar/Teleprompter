# A second sample script: shows switching scripts, and a different cast (MARA, TOM).
# Music cues work in every script; tracks are shared from public/music/.

[A lighthouse at dusk. MARA climbs the last steps. Music: calm.]

MARA: The lamp is out again.
TOM: It was lit when I left.
(Tom checks the lamp.)
TOM: The glass is cold. Nobody's been here.
MARA: Then who left the door open?
[Wind rises. Music: tense.]
TOM: We should go.
MARA: Not yet.
[Music: stop.]
